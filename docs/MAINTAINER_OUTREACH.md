# Maintainer Outreach & Backlink Engine 🌐

A structured outreach guide and copy-paste templates to notify authors of 15 fast-growing open-source tools that they are featured in the **2026 AI Coding Landscape Map** and catalog. Maintainers of rising tools consistently star, retweet, and link back to high-grade taxonomies that showcase their work.

---

## 1. Quick Backlink Snippet for Maintainers

Maintainers can embed this badge on their project README:

```markdown
[![Featured in Awesome AI Coding Tools](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mahmoudsajjadi/awesome-ai-coding-tools/main/assets/badge-featured.svg)](https://github.com/mahmoudsajjadi/awesome-ai-coding-tools)
```

## 4. Upstream Listing Checklist (`sindresorhus/awesome`)

Once the repository matures past 30 days:
1. Fork `sindresorhus/awesome`.
2. Add under `Development / Tools`:
   ```markdown
   - [AI Coding Tools](https://github.com/mahmoudsajjadi/awesome-ai-coding-tools) — Curated 3-tier taxonomy of coding assistants, CLI agents, local LLMs, and SWE-bench research.
   ```
3. Verify alphabetical ordering and open the upstream PR.
